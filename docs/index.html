Privacy by design. Your video is processed in this browser tab. It is not uploaded or recorded by Vanish Now. Camera access requires a secure HTTPS connection and your permission.

Compatibility & limitations +

Vanish Now uses standard browser camera APIs and Canvas. Chrome, Edge, Safari, and Firefox work best on a secure HTTPS site. Results depend on steady lighting and a stationary camera. This browser version does not create an OBS or Windows virtual camera output, and it cannot access your camera until you grant permission.
